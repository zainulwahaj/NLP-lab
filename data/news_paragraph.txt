Nasir Electronics said on Tuesday that it would ship to over 40 countries by March 2027. The company, based in Karachi, raised 12 million dollars last year and plans to launch a new tablet in Lahore this spring. The launch is expected to add roughly 15 percent to annual revenue. Chief executive Amina Rao told Reuters that the Pixel 10 and the iPhone remain the main competitors, and that Nasir will ship units from a new plant near Islamabad before the end of 2027.
